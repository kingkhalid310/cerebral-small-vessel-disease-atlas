# Question and contradiction governance

## Unit of maintenance

The primary maintained unit is an answerable research question. Claims, hypotheses, studies, cohorts, tools, and observations link to questions rather than floating as isolated notes.

## Evidence status

- `bounded`: a defensible answer exists only within a stated context.
- `emerging`: coherent evidence exists, but validation, causality, or transportability remains incomplete.
- `critical_gap`: the question is important and cannot yet be answered reliably.

Status is not a quality score. A bounded answer may be high-confidence within a narrow population, while a broad question remains unresolved.

## Independence rule

Consistency must be judged across independent participant sets, not publication counts. Cohort reuse, nested autopsy subsets, shared derivation data, and repeated consortium analyses must be visible in `cohorts.csv` and `study_cohorts.csv`.

## Contradiction rule

Every central hypothesis must state evidence for, evidence against, plausible alternatives, a falsifier, and the most decisive feasible experiment. Absence of a challenge record is treated as incomplete review, not consensus.

## Diagnostic chain

Technical repeatability, biological validity, diagnostic accuracy, prognostic validity, incremental value, transportability, and clinical utility are separate properties. No property is inherited automatically from another.

## Public boundary

No patient-level data, protected identifiers, local crosswalks, unpublished cohort results, or redistributed copyrighted PDFs belong in the public release. Report-derived signals remain candidate phenotype evidence until direct image and pathology validation.
