# UC007: Reduced CVR with WMH progression

## Scenario

A research participant has low CVR and increasing WMH over one year.

## Reasoning

This supports vascular dysfunction as a risk marker for tissue progression, while etiology and causal reversibility remain unresolved.

## Do not conclude

Do not infer CAA or arteriolosclerosis specificity from CVR alone.
