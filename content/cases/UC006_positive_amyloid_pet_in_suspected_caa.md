# UC006: Positive amyloid PET in suspected CAA

## Scenario

An older patient with cognitive impairment has positive amyloid PET and a small lobar microbleed burden.

## Reasoning

PET confirms fibrillar amyloid but not its vascular compartment. Integrate regional PET, tau/CSF, MRI pattern, and mixed-pathology probability.

## Do not conclude

Do not call a positive scan diagnostic of CAA.
