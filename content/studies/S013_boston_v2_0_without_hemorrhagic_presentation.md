# S013: Boston v2.0 without hemorrhagic presentation

- **Citation:** Boston Criteria v2.0 for Cerebral Amyloid Angiopathy Without Hemorrhage: An MRI-Neuropathologic Validation Study (2024)
- **Reference:** R052
- **Design:** MRI-autopsy diagnostic accuracy
- **Population:** Community and memory-clinic participants without symptomatic ICH
- **N:** 54
- **Setting:** Mayo Clinic Study of Aging and ADRC

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** Boston v1.5 and v2.0
- **Reference standard:** Moderate-to-severe lobar autopsy CAA
- **Outcome:** Diagnostic performance; 28 participants had pathology-proven CAA

## Result

Probable v2.0 sensitivity 28.6%, specificity 65.3%, AUC 0.47; any CAA sensitivity 75.0%, specificity 38.5%

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Small sample and wide confidence intervals; spectrum differs from derivation population; MRI-to-autopsy interval
- **Framework:** QUADAS-3
- **Provisional risk of bias:** high/imprecise
- **Applicability:** Highly relevant warning for asymptomatic or cognitive-only use
