# S011: Deep-learning quantitative histopathology models

- **Citation:** Deep learning assisted quantitative assessment of histopathological markers of Alzheimer's disease and cerebral amyloid angiopathy (2021)
- **Reference:** R016
- **Design:** methods development and validation
- **Population:** Digitized slides from 47 AD, CAA, and control autopsy cases
- **N:** 47
- **Setting:** Multiple autopsy sources

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** Six CNN models for plaques, vascular amyloid, iron/calcium, astrocytes, microglia, and fibrin
- **Reference standard:** Three expert human raters and prior semiquantitative scores
- **Outcome:** Pixel/object detection performance and biological analyses

## Result

Validation was good to excellent for marker models and enabled continuous mixed-effects analyses

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Training data small and staining/platform specific; human annotations are imperfect reference; external laboratory transport not established
- **Framework:** methods-validation prompts
- **Provisional risk of bias:** some concerns
- **Applicability:** Research quantification workflow, not autonomous diagnosis
