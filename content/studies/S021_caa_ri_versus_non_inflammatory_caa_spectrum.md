# S021: CAA-ri versus non-inflammatory CAA spectrum

- **Citation:** Clinical-radiological spectrum of cerebral amyloid angiopathy-related inflammation (2026)
- **Reference:** R061
- **Design:** retrospective comparative cohort
- **Population:** CAA-ri compared with non-inflammatory sporadic CAA
- **N:** 195 (37 CAA-ri; 158 CAA)
- **Setting:** Specialist referral cohort

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** Clinical and standardized MRI feature review
- **Reference standard:** Clinical CAA-ri classification; pathology available only in a subset
- **Outcome:** Frequency of ARIA-E-like and hemorrhagic features

## Result

Parenchymal hyperintensity, sulcal hyperintensity, and gyral swelling were far more common in CAA-ri than non-inflammatory CAA; CAA-ri also had more numerous lobar microbleeds.

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Retrospective referral sample, modest CAA-ri count, incorporation/circularity risk, and incomplete pathology verification.
- **Framework:** QUADAS-3 concepts
- **Provisional risk of bias:** some concerns
- **Applicability:** Useful for differential recognition in specialist settings; external prospective accuracy remains needed.
