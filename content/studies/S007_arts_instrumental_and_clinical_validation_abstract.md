# S007: ARTS instrumental and clinical validation abstract

- **Citation:** The ARTS marker of arteriolosclerosis: instrumental and clinical validation (2024)
- **Reference:** R018
- **Design:** conference-supplement abstract
- **Population:** Older adults in multisite technical datasets and Rush/ARIC cognitive cohorts
- **N:** 20 inter-rater; 41 retest; 20 four-scanner; 156 Rush; 906 ARIC
- **Setting:** MarkVCID consortium

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** Containerized ARTS score
- **Reference standard:** Technical repeatability/reproducibility; cognitive trajectories rather than pathology for clinical validation
- **Outcome:** Inter-rater reliability, test-retest repeatability, inter-scanner reproducibility, and cognitive association

## Result

ICC 0.9999 inter-rater, 0.996 test-retest, 0.955 inter-scanner; baseline ARTS associated with cognitive level and decline

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Supplement abstract rather than complete report; limited appraisal detail; cognitive association does not revalidate pathology specificity
- **Framework:** PROBAST
- **Provisional risk of bias:** unclear/high
- **Applicability:** Strong technical signal; biological and clinical utility remain separate
