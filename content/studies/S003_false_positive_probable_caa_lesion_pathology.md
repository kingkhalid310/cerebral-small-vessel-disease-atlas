# S003: False-positive probable CAA lesion pathology

- **Citation:** Histopathological correlates of lobar microbleeds in false-positive cerebral amyloid angiopathy cases (2023)
- **Reference:** R015
- **Design:** MRI-guided lesion-level autopsy case series
- **Population:** Three patients meeting probable CAA during life but without CAA on routine neuropathology
- **N:** 3
- **Setting:** MGH brain donation program

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** Lobar CMB location on in vivo, ex vivo 3T, and selected 7T MRI
- **Reference standard:** Serial histopathology of targeted lesions and culprit vessels
- **Outcome:** Pathologic substrate of lobar CMBs

## Result

Seven true CMB correlates were retrieved; culprit vessels showed moderate/severe arteriolosclerosis and no CAA; lesions tended toward juxtacortical/subcortical WM

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Extreme selected case series; only three patients and seven confirmed CMBs; localization hypothesis exploratory
- **Framework:** pathology case-series prompts
- **Provisional risk of bias:** high
- **Applicability:** Demonstrates counterexamples, not frequency or diagnostic accuracy
