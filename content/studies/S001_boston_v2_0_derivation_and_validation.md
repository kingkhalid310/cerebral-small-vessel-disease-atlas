# S001: Boston v2.0 derivation and validation

- **Citation:** The Boston criteria version 2.0 for cerebral amyloid angiopathy (2022)
- **Reference:** R006
- **Design:** multicentre retrospective diagnostic accuracy
- **Population:** Age >=50 with spontaneous ICH, cognitive impairment, or TFNE and MRI plus tissue
- **N:** 341
- **Setting:** 10 North American and European academic centres

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** Boston v2.0 MRI-clinical criteria
- **Reference standard:** Neuropathologic CAA assessment from biopsy, hematoma evacuation, or autopsy
- **Outcome:** Probable CAA sensitivity, specificity, and AUC

## Result

Derivation 74.8%/84.6%; temporal validation 92.5%/89.5%; geographic validation 80.2%/81.5%; full-autopsy subset 74.5%/95.0%, AUC 0.848

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Retrospective tissue-selected sample; heterogeneous tissue extent and clinical presentations; not validated as population screening
- **Framework:** QUADAS-3
- **Provisional risk of bias:** some concerns
- **Applicability:** Strongest for symptomatic referral populations resembling enrolled cases
