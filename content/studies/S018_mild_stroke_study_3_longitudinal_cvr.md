# S018: Mild Stroke Study 3 longitudinal CVR

- **Citation:** Association of cerebrovascular reactivity with 1-year imaging and clinical outcomes in small vessel disease: an observational cohort study (2024)
- **Reference:** R060
- **Design:** prospective observational cohort
- **Population:** Patients with minor ischemic stroke enriched for MRI SVD features
- **N:** 163 follow-up analyses
- **Setting:** Edinburgh/Lothian stroke services

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** Baseline BOLD CVR during 6% carbon-dioxide challenge
- **Reference standard:** One-year quantitative MRI progression and clinical outcomes
- **Outcome:** WMH/PVS progression and clinical outcomes

## Result

Lower baseline CVR in normal-appearing tissue predicted greater WMH and PVS progression after adjustment; CVR was not associated with 1-year clinical outcomes.

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Stroke-enriched single-region cohort, one-year horizon, multiple tissue/outcome analyses, and no direct vessel-wall pathology.
- **Framework:** QUIPS
- **Provisional risk of bias:** some concerns
- **Applicability:** Supports temporal prediction of tissue injury in SVD but not etiologic classification.
