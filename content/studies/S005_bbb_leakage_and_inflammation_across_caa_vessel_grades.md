# S005: BBB leakage and inflammation across CAA vessel grades

- **Citation:** Blood-brain barrier leakage and perivascular inflammation in cerebral amyloid angiopathy (2022)
- **Reference:** R013
- **Design:** cross-sectional vessel-level autopsy study
- **Population:** Seven definite CAA cases: two MRI-targeted severe cases and five consecutive cases
- **N:** 7
- **Setting:** MGH

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** CAA vessel grade
- **Reference standard:** Immunohistochemistry for fibrin(ogen), SMA, GFAP, and CD68
- **Outcome:** Vessel-wall leakage, smooth-muscle loss, inflammation, and remodeling

## Result

Leakage and SMA loss were more frequent at mild amyloid grade than grade 0; all remodeled grade 3/4 vessels showed leakage; inflammation predominated later

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Seven cases; many vessel observations are not independent persons; temporal order inferred from cross-sectional grades
- **Framework:** pathology-mechanism prompts
- **Provisional risk of bias:** high
- **Applicability:** Mechanistic hypothesis generation; not proof that leakage initiates rupture
