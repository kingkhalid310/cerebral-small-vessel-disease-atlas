# S006: ARTS development and pathology testing

- **Citation:** ARTS: a novel in-vivo classifier of arteriolosclerosis for the older adult brain (2021)
- **Reference:** R017
- **Design:** prediction model development, translation, and external cohort association
- **Population:** Community-based older adults with ex vivo MRI/pathology plus separate in vivo test and cognitive cohorts
- **N:** 119 training; 79 pathology test; 369, 72, and 244 cognitive cohorts
- **Setting:** MAP/ROS, MARS, ADNI

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** WMH, regional FA, age, and sex combined in automated ARTS score
- **Reference standard:** Semiquantitative autopsy arteriolosclerosis for diagnostic testing
- **Outcome:** Pathology discrimination, reproducibility, and cognitive decline

## Result

Mean ex vivo AUC 0.78; in vivo test AUC 0.79 when MRI-death interval <=2.4 years; scan-rescan ICC 0.99; higher score associated with decline in selected cognitive domains

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Small pathology samples; performance worsened with longer MRI-death interval; oldest-old cohorts; calibration and clinical thresholds not established
- **Framework:** PROBAST
- **Provisional risk of bias:** high
- **Applicability:** Candidate research biomarker; not a clinical diagnostic test
