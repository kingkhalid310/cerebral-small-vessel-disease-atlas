# S004: PVS and overlying cortical CAA

- **Citation:** Perivascular space dilation is associated with vascular amyloid-beta accumulation in the overlying cortex (2022)
- **Reference:** R012
- **Design:** spatial ex vivo MRI-histopathology study
- **Population:** Autopsy cases with definite CAA and non-CAA controls
- **N:** 24
- **Setting:** MGH brain donation program

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** In vivo/ex vivo MRI-visible PVS and segmented histologic EPVS
- **Reference standard:** Regional and single-vessel histopathology
- **Outcome:** Association of EPVS with vascular amyloid and vessel-wall changes

## Result

EPVS were mainly around WM portions of perforating cortical arterioles; burden associated with CAA severity in overlying cortex; affected vessels showed smooth-muscle loss and vascular amyloid

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** 19 CAA and 5 controls; selected autopsy material; cross-sectional spatial association cannot establish flow direction
- **Framework:** pathology-spatial prompts
- **Provisional risk of bias:** high
- **Applicability:** Supports spatial biological plausibility; not standalone diagnostic accuracy
