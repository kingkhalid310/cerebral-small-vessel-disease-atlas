# S002: Edinburgh CT and APOE model

- **Citation:** The Edinburgh CT and genetic diagnostic criteria for lobar intracerebral haemorrhage associated with cerebral amyloid angiopathy (2018)
- **Reference:** R007
- **Design:** prospective population-based inception cohort; model development and diagnostic accuracy
- **Population:** Adults with first-ever CT-diagnosed ICH who died and underwent research autopsy
- **N:** 110
- **Setting:** Lothian, Scotland

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** CT subarachnoid extension, finger-like projections, and APOE e4
- **Reference standard:** Masked standardized neuropathology
- **Outcome:** CAA-associated lobar ICH

## Result

Among 62 lobar ICH cases, 36 had moderate/severe CAA; three-variable model c-statistic 0.92; rule-in specificity 96%

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Only 62 lobar ICH cases; internal bootstrap validation; genotype may not be available; restricted to lobar ICH
- **Framework:** QUADAS-3
- **Provisional risk of bias:** some concerns
- **Applicability:** Applicable to acute lobar ICH evaluated by CT; not other CAA presentations
