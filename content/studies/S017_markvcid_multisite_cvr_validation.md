# S017: MarkVCID multisite CVR validation

- **Citation:** Cerebrovascular reactivity MRI as a biomarker for cerebral small vessel disease-related cognitive decline: Multi-site validation in the MarkVCID Consortium (2024)
- **Reference:** R054
- **Design:** predefined multisite validation study
- **Population:** Older adults enrolled at three MarkVCID analysis sites
- **N:** 263
- **Setting:** Three US research sites

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** BOLD MRI CVR during 5% carbon-dioxide inhalation
- **Reference standard:** Concurrent MoCA and executive-function item-response score
- **Outcome:** Cross-site association with cognition

## Result

CVR was positively associated with MoCA at every site and with executive function in the combined analysis.

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Association with cognition is not pathology validation, diagnostic accuracy, or proof of etiologic specificity; gas challenge limits scalability.
- **Framework:** QUIPS/biomarker validation
- **Provisional risk of bias:** some concerns
- **Applicability:** Supports a physiological VCID biomarker, not a CAA- or arteriolosclerosis-specific test.
