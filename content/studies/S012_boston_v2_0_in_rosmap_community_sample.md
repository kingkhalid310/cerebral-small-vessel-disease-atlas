# S012: Boston v2.0 in ROSMAP community sample

- **Citation:** Sensitivity and Specificity of the Boston Criteria Version 2.0 for the Diagnosis of Cerebral Amyloid Angiopathy in a Community-Based Sample (2024)
- **Reference:** R051
- **Design:** community-based MRI-autopsy diagnostic accuracy
- **Population:** Deceased ROSMAP participants with in vivo 3T MRI and detailed autopsy
- **N:** 134
- **Setting:** Longitudinal community cohorts

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** Boston v1.0, v1.5, and v2.0
- **Reference standard:** Moderate-to-severe autopsy CAA
- **Outcome:** Diagnostic performance; 49 participants had pathology-proven CAA

## Result

Probable v2.0 sensitivity 38.8%, specificity 83.5%, PPV 57.6%, NPV 70.3%; cortical CMB count distinguished true from false positives

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Only 134 of a much larger autopsy cohort had usable in vivo MRI; older selected sample; interval and sequence limitations
- **Framework:** QUADAS-3
- **Provisional risk of bias:** some concerns/high selection
- **Applicability:** Directly relevant to community-dwelling older adults; not identical to clinical referrals
