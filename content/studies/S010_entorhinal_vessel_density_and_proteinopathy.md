# S010: Entorhinal vessel density and proteinopathy

- **Citation:** Entorhinal vessel density correlates with phosphorylated tau and TDP-43 pathology (2024)
- **Reference:** R035
- **Design:** ex vivo 7T MRI and histopathology spatial association
- **Population:** Twelve human postmortem medial temporal lobe samples
- **N:** 12
- **Setting:** MGH

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** Manually labelled vessels on 100-micron ex vivo MRI
- **Reference standard:** Semiquantitative p-tau and TDP-43 pathology
- **Outcome:** Regional vessel density and pathology

## Result

Perirhinal cortex was more vascularized than entorhinal cortex; vessel density correlated with p-tau and TDP-43; severe preclinical tau regions had higher density

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Twelve cases; cross-sectional; manual tracing; higher density can reflect baseline anatomy, remodeling, or selection
- **Framework:** pathology-spatial prompts
- **Provisional risk of bias:** high
- **Applicability:** Exploratory spatial biology, not a diagnostic biomarker
