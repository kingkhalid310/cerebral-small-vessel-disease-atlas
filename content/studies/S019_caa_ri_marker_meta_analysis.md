# S019: CAA-ri marker meta-analysis

- **Citation:** Clinical, neuroimaging, and genetic markers in cerebral amyloid angiopathy-related inflammation: a systematic review and meta-analysis (2023)
- **Reference:** R057
- **Design:** systematic review and random-effects meta-analysis
- **Population:** Probable or definite CAA-ri across 21 cohorts
- **N:** 378
- **Setting:** Mostly retrospective specialist cohorts

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** Clinical, MRI, genetic, and CSF features
- **Reference standard:** Probable/definite CAA-ri with variable biopsy availability
- **Outcome:** Pooled prevalence of presentation and imaging markers

## Result

Cognitive decline occurred in 70%; FLAIR white-matter lesions in 98%; lobar microbleeds in 96%; cSS in 51%; estimates for many features were heterogeneous.

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Predominantly retrospective reports, variable definitions and biopsy verification, selection bias, and high heterogeneity.
- **Framework:** AMSTAR-2/QUADAS concepts
- **Provisional risk of bias:** high or unclear in component evidence
- **Applicability:** Describes the recognized CAA-ri phenotype; does not by itself validate a treatment rule.
