# S020: Regional amyloid PET meta-analysis

- **Citation:** Cerebral amyloid angiopathy and amyloid load distribution detected on amyloid-positron emission tomography: a systematic review and meta-analysis (2025)
- **Reference:** R059
- **Design:** systematic review and meta-analysis
- **Population:** CAA, AD, hypertensive ICH, and healthy-control groups
- **N:** 16 cohorts; 271 CAA participants
- **Setting:** Prospective observational imaging cohorts

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** Global and regional amyloid PET retention
- **Reference standard:** Predominantly clinical CAA criteria; limited pathology correlation
- **Outcome:** Group differences in global and regional tracer retention

## Result

CAA showed lower global retention than AD, higher retention than hypertensive ICH/controls, and relatively higher occipital-to-global uptake than AD.

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Most CAA cases met probable clinical criteria rather than pathology confirmation; only two tracers dominated; threshold and timing heterogeneity.
- **Framework:** AMSTAR-2/diagnostic applicability
- **Provisional risk of bias:** some concerns
- **Applicability:** Supports regional signal as a research candidate, not a standalone diagnostic threshold.
