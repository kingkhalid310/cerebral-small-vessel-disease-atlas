# S008: ARTS vascular risk and incident outcomes

- **Citation:** ARTS is associated with vascular risk factors, MCI, dementia, and stroke (2025)
- **Reference:** R019
- **Design:** longitudinal observational cohort
- **Population:** Diverse older adults without dementia at baseline
- **N:** 1226
- **Setting:** MAP, MARS, AA Core, LATC, and ROS

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** Baseline ARTS score
- **Reference standard:** Standard cohort adjudication of MCI, dementia, stroke, and vascular risks
- **Outcome:** Incident MCI, dementia, stroke, and risk-factor associations

## Result

Higher ARTS score was associated with incident MCI, dementia, stroke, and several vascular risk factors

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Observational associations; score includes age/sex and MRI injury features; outcome association does not prove arteriolosclerosis specificity or treatment responsiveness
- **Framework:** cohort prompts
- **Provisional risk of bias:** some concerns
- **Applicability:** Supports prognostic association in older adults, not clinical diagnosis
