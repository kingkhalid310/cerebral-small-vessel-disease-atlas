# S009: Arteriolosclerosis and hippocampal sclerosis of ageing

- **Citation:** Arteriolosclerosis that affects multiple brain regions is linked to hippocampal sclerosis of ageing (2014)
- **Reference:** R033
- **Design:** multi-cohort autopsy association with digital pathology subset
- **Population:** Autopsied adults age >=80 across UK-ADC, NACC, and Nun Study
- **N:** 2018
- **Setting:** Three autopsy cohorts

## Measurement and reference

- **Index test or exposure:** Regional semiquantitative arteriolosclerosis and vessel morphology
- **Reference standard:** Neuropathologic HS-ageing
- **Outcome:** Association and vessel morphology

## Result

226 HS-ageing cases and 1792 controls; arteriolosclerosis across multiple regions associated with HS-ageing; digital subset showed thicker/larger SMA-positive arterioles

## Limitations and provisional appraisal

- **Major limitations:** Autopsy selection; historical semiquantitative ratings; cross-sectional association; HS-ageing terminology predates current LATE framework
- **Framework:** autopsy-cohort prompts
- **Provisional risk of bias:** some concerns
- **Applicability:** Strong association evidence; causal direction unresolved
