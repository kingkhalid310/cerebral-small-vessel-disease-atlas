# DP007: ARIA monitoring framework

- **Context:** Safety monitoring during anti-amyloid therapy
- **Evidence grade:** moderate

## Validation chain

- **Technical validity:** Standardized serial MRI and communication are central
- **Biological validity:** ARIA-E and ARIA-H reflect therapy-associated permeability/inflammatory and hemorrhagic injury
- **Diagnostic accuracy:** Designed for treatment context, not spontaneous CAA diagnosis
- **Prognostic validity:** Baseline markers inform risk; severe-event prediction remains limited
- **Incremental value:** Provides operational safety value beyond unsystematic MRI review
- **Transportability:** Therapy-, protocol-, and healthcare-system dependent
- **Clinical utility:** High within current treatment pathways using drug-specific guidance

- **Main failure mode:** Confusing ARIA with infarct, PRES, spontaneous CAA-ri, or other mimics
- **Next validation:** Large real-world registry with standardized MRI and adjudicated severe outcomes
- **References:** R040;R055
