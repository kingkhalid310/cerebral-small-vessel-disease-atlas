# DP005: CAA-ri clinicoradiologic criteria

- **Context:** Recognition of inflammatory CAA syndrome
- **Evidence grade:** moderate

## Validation chain

- **Technical validity:** Requires appropriate FLAIR, susceptibility, diffusion, and often contrast imaging
- **Biological validity:** Phenotype reflects inflammatory/permeability injury around amyloid-laden vessels
- **Diagnostic accuracy:** Validated in selected cohorts; mimic-rich external performance remains uncertain
- **Prognostic validity:** Not a complete treatment-response or relapse model
- **Incremental value:** Can reduce unnecessary biopsy when syndrome and imaging align
- **Transportability:** Best in specialist contexts with alternative-diagnosis assessment
- **Clinical utility:** Clinically useful with expert evaluation

- **Main failure mode:** Missing infection, neoplasm, PRES, vasculitis, or therapy-associated ARIA
- **Next validation:** Prospective blinded accuracy study including realistic mimics
- **References:** R008;R057;R061
