# DP003: CVR MRI

- **Context:** Physiological measurement of vascular reserve in SVD/VCID
- **Evidence grade:** moderate

## Validation chain

- **Technical validity:** Multisite protocol evidence exists; acquisition and gas delivery remain demanding
- **Biological validity:** Measures vascular responsiveness and predicts some structural progression
- **Diagnostic accuracy:** No pathology-specific CAA or arteriolosclerosis threshold
- **Prognostic validity:** Associated with cognition and one-year WMH/PVS progression; not all clinical outcomes
- **Incremental value:** Needs comparison with conventional MRI and vascular risks
- **Transportability:** Promising across research sites; routine-care feasibility limited
- **Clinical utility:** Research only

- **Main failure mode:** Treating a general vascular-function signal as an etiologic diagnosis
- **Next validation:** Pathology-linked and intervention-responsive multisite qualification
- **References:** R054;R060
