# DP002: ARTS

- **Context:** Research prediction of pathology-linked arteriolosclerosis risk
- **Evidence grade:** low

## Validation chain

- **Technical validity:** Promising repeatability evidence, including conference-level validation
- **Biological validity:** Development linked MRI/demographic features to autopsy pathology
- **Diagnostic accuracy:** Development AUC reported; independent external pathology accuracy not established
- **Prognostic validity:** Associations with cognition and vascular outcomes reported
- **Incremental value:** Not yet demonstrated against strong conventional baselines across independent cohorts
- **Transportability:** Uncertain beyond ROSMAP/ADNI-related research settings
- **Clinical utility:** Not established

- **Main failure mode:** Conflating reproducibility or outcome association with diagnostic validation
- **Next validation:** Locked independent MRI-autopsy validation with calibration and decision analysis
- **References:** R017;R018;R019
