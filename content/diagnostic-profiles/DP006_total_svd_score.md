# DP006: Total SVD score

- **Context:** Compact summary of MRI burden
- **Evidence grade:** moderate

## Validation chain

- **Technical validity:** Simple and reproducible when component ratings are reliable
- **Biological validity:** Combines downstream injury markers from potentially different etiologies
- **Diagnostic accuracy:** Not intended to diagnose a vessel pathology
- **Prognostic validity:** Associated with clinical and network outcomes in several cohorts
- **Incremental value:** May summarize burden efficiently but can discard spatial and continuous information
- **Transportability:** Reasonable for burden comparisons with harmonized definitions
- **Clinical utility:** Descriptive/prognostic research adjunct

- **Main failure mode:** Interpreting a burden score as CAA or arteriolosclerosis
- **Next validation:** Outcome-specific comparison with continuous and spatial measures
- **References:** R027;R028;R029
