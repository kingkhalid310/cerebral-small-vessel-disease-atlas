# H004: A strictly lobar hemorrhagic pattern is sufficiently specific for CAA

- **Level:** diagnostic
- **Current confidence:** moderate

## Strongest evidence for

Strong pathology-linked enrichment and established Boston criteria performance in symptomatic cohorts

## Strongest evidence against

Nonamyloid arteriolosclerosis can produce some lobar/juxtacortical microbleeds; performance falls in other spectra

## Alternative explanations

Trauma, embolic lesions, vascular malformations, treatment effects, and mixed disease

## Falsifier

High rate of non-CAA pathology among rigorously localized strictly lobar lesions

## Decisive experiment

Lesion-matched MRI-histology plus external diagnostic validation

**Claims:** C004;C017  
**References:** R006;R015;R051;R052
