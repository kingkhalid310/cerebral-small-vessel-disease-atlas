# H008: ARTS is a valid in-vivo biomarker of arteriolosclerosis

- **Level:** prediction biomarker
- **Current confidence:** low

## Strongest evidence for

Pathology-linked development, reported AUC, translation to in-vivo MRI, and repeatability work

## Strongest evidence against

Limited independent pathology validation, selected cohorts, and uncertain incremental utility

## Alternative explanations

ARTS may reflect general aging, WMH/diffusion injury, or vascular risk burden

## Falsifier

Failure of locked calibration/discrimination in an independent MRI-autopsy cohort

## Decisive experiment

Independent preregistered external pathology validation against strong baselines

**Claims:** C011;C012;C013
