# H005: CSO-PVS are an in-vivo marker of regional cortical CAA burden

- **Level:** biomarker
- **Current confidence:** moderate

## Strongest evidence for

Spatial MRI-pathology association with overlying cortical amyloid and smooth-muscle loss

## Strongest evidence against

PVS are common with aging and multiple SVD mechanisms; rating and causal interpretation vary

## Alternative explanations

Atrophy, altered interstitial fluid, venous factors, and other small-vessel pathology

## Falsifier

No regional association in larger blinded pathology cohorts after confounding control

## Decisive experiment

Multiregion MRI-autopsy mapping with reproducible automated PVS quantification
