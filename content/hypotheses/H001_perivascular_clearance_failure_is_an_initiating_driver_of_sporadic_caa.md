# H001: Perivascular clearance failure is an initiating driver of sporadic CAA

- **Level:** mechanism
- **Current confidence:** moderate

## Strongest evidence for

Anatomical plausibility, vascular amyloid distribution, hereditary/experimental models, and spatial PVS-pathology associations

## Strongest evidence against

Human flow direction and temporal onset are not directly established; clearance impairment may be secondary to vessel injury

## Alternative explanations

Production, vessel affinity, age-related matrix change, pulsatility, and BBB dysfunction

## Falsifier

Normal direct clearance before and during early vascular amyloid accumulation despite sensitive measurement

## Decisive experiment

Longitudinal compartment-specific human transport study beginning before imaging disease

**Claims:** C001  
**References:** R010;R011;R012
