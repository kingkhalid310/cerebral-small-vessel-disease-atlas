# H010: Regional amyloid PET can distinguish CAA from AD

- **Level:** diagnostic biomarker
- **Current confidence:** low

## Strongest evidence for

Meta-analysis finds lower global CAA uptake than AD and relatively higher occipital-to-global uptake

## Strongest evidence against

Most reference diagnoses are clinical; AD co-pathology and tracer heterogeneity blur compartments

## Alternative explanations

Disease stage, tracer affinity, atrophy, and mixed parenchymal/vascular amyloid

## Falsifier

No patient-level separation against compartment-specific autopsy pathology

## Decisive experiment

Prospective multi-tracer PET-to-autopsy threshold validation

**Claims:** C021  
**References:** R032;R059
