# H007: Arteriolosclerosis independently causes cognitive decline

- **Level:** causal outcome
- **Current confidence:** moderate

## Strongest evidence for

Autopsy associations persist after adjustment in several aging cohorts

## Strongest evidence against

Residual confounding, measurement error, mediation by infarcts/WMH, and cohort reuse

## Alternative explanations

ADNC, LATE, hippocampal sclerosis, frailty, and vascular risks

## Falsifier

No association after robust measurement-error and causal-pathway modeling across independent cohorts

## Decisive experiment

Replicated longitudinal autopsy mediation analysis with harmonized pathology

**Claims:** C008;C009  
**References:** R003;R033;R049;R050
