# H003: BBB leakage is an early causal amplifier of CAA vessel injury

- **Level:** mechanism
- **Current confidence:** moderate

## Strongest evidence for

Leakage and inflammation increase with histologic vessel injury and fit ARIA/CAA-ri biology

## Strongest evidence against

Human evidence is mainly cross-sectional and leakage can follow vessel damage

## Alternative explanations

Inflammation, ischemia, and amyloid toxicity independently generate permeability

## Falsifier

No leakage before progression when measured serially with sensitive methods

## Decisive experiment

Serial DCE-MRI/fluid markers with spatial pathology and treatment perturbation

**Claims:** C003;C022  
**References:** R013;R055
