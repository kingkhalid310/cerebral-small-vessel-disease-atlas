# T003: Boston criteria v2.0

- **Category:** diagnostic criteria
- **Intended context:** Estimate likelihood of sporadic CAA in adults >=50 with relevant clinical presentation
- **Target construct:** CAA causing clinical/imaging phenotype
- **Required inputs:** Clinical presentation, MRI hemorrhagic markers, absence of deep hemorrhagic lesions, selected WM features
- **Output:** Possible/probable CAA categories

## Evidence and use

- **Development/authority:** International CAA Association study
- **Validation status:** Validated in symptomatic temporal/geographic cohorts; lower performance in community/nonhemorrhagic samples
- **Reference standard:** Neuropathologic CAA
- **Key strength:** Best established noninvasive CAA framework
- **Failure mode:** Spectrum dependence; mimics; sequence sensitivity; not population screening
- **Clinical status:** Clinical and research classification with specialist interpretation
- **Version/access:** v2.0
- **References:** R006;R051;R052
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-18
