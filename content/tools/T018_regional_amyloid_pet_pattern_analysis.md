# T018: Regional amyloid PET pattern analysis

- **Category:** molecular imaging biomarker
- **Intended context:** research differential assessment of CAA, AD, and controls
- **Target construct:** Global and regional fibrillar amyloid tracer retention
- **Required inputs:** Amyloid PET, tracer-specific processing, reference region, structural imaging, prespecified regional ratios
- **Output:** Global uptake and regional distribution metrics

## Evidence and use

- **Development/authority:** Observational PET cohorts and meta-analyses
- **Validation status:** Group-level differences; no universal pathology-validated patient threshold
- **Reference standard:** Usually clinical CAA/AD classifications; sparse pathology
- **Key strength:** Directly probes fibrillar amyloid distribution in vivo
- **Failure mode:** Cannot reliably assign vascular versus parenchymal amyloid in mixed disease
- **Clinical status:** Research adjunct; not standalone CAA diagnosis
- **Version/access:** Tracer and pipeline specific
- **References:** R032;R059
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-18
