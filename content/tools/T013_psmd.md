# T013: PSMD

- **Category:** diffusion MRI biomarker
- **Intended context:** Quantify diffuse white-matter microstructural injury
- **Target construct:** Peak width of skeletonized mean diffusivity
- **Required inputs:** Diffusion MRI and processing pipeline
- **Output:** Continuous PSMD

## Evidence and use

- **Development/authority:** MarkVCID and prior methodological studies
- **Validation status:** Multisite instrumental and biological validation
- **Reference standard:** Reproducibility and clinical association
- **Key strength:** Automated, sensitive continuous measure
- **Failure mode:** Not etiologically specific; acquisition and processing dependence
- **Clinical status:** Research biomarker
- **Version/access:** Version-locked pipeline required
- **References:** R021;R022
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-18
