# T017: Carbon-dioxide challenge cerebrovascular reactivity MRI

- **Category:** physiological imaging biomarker
- **Intended context:** research measurement of vascular reserve and dysfunction
- **Target construct:** Change in cerebral MRI signal or flow response to a vasoactive challenge
- **Required inputs:** Standardized BOLD or perfusion MRI, gas delivery/end-tidal monitoring, preprocessing, region definition
- **Output:** Regional or voxelwise CVR magnitude and related parameters

## Evidence and use

- **Development/authority:** MarkVCID and other SVD research programs
- **Validation status:** Multisite cognitive association and longitudinal imaging prediction; pathology specificity untested
- **Reference standard:** Reproducibility, cognition, and longitudinal SVD imaging rather than vessel pathology
- **Key strength:** Measures vascular function before irreversible structural injury
- **Failure mode:** Gas challenge, analysis, motion, vascular anatomy, and systemic physiology reduce scalability and specificity
- **Clinical status:** Research biomarker
- **Version/access:** Protocol-dependent
- **References:** R054;R060;R020;R021
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-18
