# T014: White-matter free water

- **Category:** diffusion MRI biomarker
- **Intended context:** Measure extracellular/free-water-related tissue change
- **Target construct:** Diffuse white-matter microstructural injury
- **Required inputs:** Multishell or model-appropriate diffusion MRI
- **Output:** Voxel/region continuous free-water measure

## Evidence and use

- **Development/authority:** MarkVCID biomarker program
- **Validation status:** Instrumental/biological validation evolving
- **Reference standard:** Repeatability and clinical association
- **Key strength:** Potential sensitivity to subtle injury
- **Failure mode:** Model and motion dependence; nonspecific biology
- **Clinical status:** Research biomarker
- **Version/access:** Record model and software version
- **References:** R020;R021
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-18
