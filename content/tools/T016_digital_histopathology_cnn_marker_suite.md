# T016: Digital histopathology CNN marker suite

- **Category:** digital pathology method
- **Intended context:** Quantify vascular amyloid, plaques, iron/calcium, glia, and fibrin
- **Target construct:** Histopathologic marker burden
- **Required inputs:** Digitized stained slides and trained Aiforia models
- **Output:** Continuous marker areas/counts

## Evidence and use

- **Development/authority:** Perosa et al. 2021
- **Validation status:** Good-to-excellent internal expert-referenced performance
- **Reference standard:** Expert annotations and prior scores
- **Key strength:** Enables continuous spatial pathology analyses
- **Failure mode:** Laboratory/stain/platform domain shift; external validation limited
- **Clinical status:** Research only
- **Version/access:** Aiforia workflow/model-specific
- **References:** R016
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-18
