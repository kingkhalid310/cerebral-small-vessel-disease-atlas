# T009: CAA-SVD score

- **Category:** ordinal burden score
- **Intended context:** Summarize CAA-oriented MRI burden
- **Target construct:** Visible MRI burden associated with CAA
- **Required inputs:** Lobar CMBs, cSS, CSO-PVS, WMH
- **Output:** 0-6 score

## Evidence and use

- **Development/authority:** CAA cohort study
- **Validation status:** Construct and outcome associations
- **Reference standard:** Clinical/pathologic variables across studies
- **Key strength:** Compact disease-burden summary
- **Failure mode:** Not a diagnostic rule; components overlap with non-CAA disease
- **Clinical status:** Research descriptive/prognostic
- **Version/access:** 2016 score
- **References:** R028;R029
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-18
