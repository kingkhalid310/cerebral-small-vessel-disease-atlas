# T005: Clinicoradiologic CAA-ri criteria

- **Category:** diagnostic criteria
- **Intended context:** Recognize probable CAA-related inflammation
- **Target construct:** Inflammatory CAA syndrome
- **Required inputs:** Subacute clinical syndrome, MRI edema/asymmetry and hemorrhagic evidence, exclusion of alternatives
- **Output:** Possible/probable CAA-ri

## Evidence and use

- **Development/authority:** Published validation study
- **Validation status:** Validated in selected specialist cohorts
- **Reference standard:** Biopsy and/or expert follow-up diagnosis
- **Key strength:** Can support timely immunotherapy evaluation
- **Failure mode:** Rare syndrome, mimics, verification and referral bias
- **Clinical status:** Clinical specialist framework
- **Version/access:** 2016 validation
- **References:** R008
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-18
