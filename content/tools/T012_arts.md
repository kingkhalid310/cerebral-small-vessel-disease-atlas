# T012: ARTS

- **Category:** automated prediction biomarker
- **Intended context:** Research prediction/enrichment for arteriolosclerosis risk
- **Target construct:** Probability-linked arteriolosclerosis signal
- **Required inputs:** T1 MRI, FLAIR/WMH, diffusion FA, age, sex, container prerequisites
- **Output:** Continuous ARTS confidence score

## Evidence and use

- **Development/authority:** Rush/IIT development; MarkVCID packaging
- **Validation status:** Pathology-linked development/test; strong reported repeatability; transportability incomplete
- **Reference standard:** Semiquantitative autopsy arteriolosclerosis for original diagnostic test
- **Key strength:** First openly available automated pathology-linked B-ASC score
- **Failure mode:** Older selected cohorts; MRI-death interval; no clinical cutoff; score is not pathology
- **Clinical status:** Research only
- **Version/access:** NITRC ARTS container; record version in every analysis
- **References:** R017;R018;R019
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-18
