# T001: STRIVE-1

- **Category:** terminology standard
- **Intended context:** research description and harmonization
- **Target construct:** MRI manifestations of cSVD
- **Required inputs:** Multisequence structural MRI
- **Output:** Standard names and definitions

## Evidence and use

- **Development/authority:** International expert consensus 2013
- **Validation status:** Widely adopted terminology
- **Reference standard:** Consensus and measurement reliability rather than etiologic pathology
- **Key strength:** Established common language
- **Failure mode:** Phenotype terms may be misused as disease labels
- **Clinical status:** Research/clinical descriptive standard
- **Version/access:** 2013 publication
- **References:** R002
