# T004: Edinburgh CT and genetic criteria

- **Category:** diagnostic prediction rule
- **Intended context:** Rule in/out CAA-associated lobar ICH when MRI is unavailable or not primary
- **Target construct:** CAA as cause of lobar ICH
- **Required inputs:** Acute CT morphology and APOE e4 genotype
- **Output:** Probability/rule-in/rule-out category

## Evidence and use

- **Development/authority:** Prospective LINCHPIN autopsy cohort
- **Validation status:** Internally validated; external validation still important
- **Reference standard:** Masked autopsy neuropathology
- **Key strength:** Uses widely available acute CT features
- **Failure mode:** Small derivation set; genotype availability; restricted to lobar ICH
- **Clinical status:** Specialist/research use
- **Version/access:** 2018 model
- **References:** R007
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-18
