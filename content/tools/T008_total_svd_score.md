# T008: Total SVD score

- **Category:** ordinal burden score
- **Intended context:** Summarize visible MRI SVD burden
- **Target construct:** Total conventional MRI lesion burden
- **Required inputs:** MRI ratings of lacunes, CMBs, WMH, and PVS
- **Output:** 0-4 score

## Evidence and use

- **Development/authority:** Stroke-cohort construct study
- **Validation status:** Multiple observational associations
- **Reference standard:** Clinical/construct outcomes
- **Key strength:** Simple and reproducible summary
- **Failure mode:** Equal weighting, thresholding, mixed etiologies
- **Clinical status:** Research descriptive/prognostic
- **Version/access:** Original 2014 implementation
- **References:** R027
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-18
