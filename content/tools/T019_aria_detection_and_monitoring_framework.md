# T019: ARIA detection and monitoring framework

- **Category:** safety monitoring framework
- **Intended context:** MRI surveillance during anti-amyloid therapy
- **Target construct:** ARIA-E edema/effusion and ARIA-H hemorrhagic abnormalities
- **Required inputs:** Baseline and serial FLAIR, diffusion, T2*-GRE or SWI, clinical context, treatment timing
- **Output:** Presence, type, severity, evolution, and action-relevant communication

## Evidence and use

- **Development/authority:** Alzheimer's Association workgroup and neuroradiology guidance
- **Validation status:** Consensus-based monitoring with therapy-specific evidence; real-world severe-event prediction remains limited
- **Reference standard:** Trial and clinical imaging definitions plus outcomes
- **Key strength:** Connects standardized detection to safety workflows
- **Failure mode:** ARIA, spontaneous CAA-ri, infarction, PRES, infection, and other mimics may overlap
- **Clinical status:** Clinical safety framework; use current drug-specific guidance
- **Version/access:** 2026 workgroup update
- **References:** R040;R055
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-18
