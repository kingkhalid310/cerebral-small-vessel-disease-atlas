# C017: Boston v2.0 accuracy is strongly dependent on clinical context

- **Domain:** diagnosis
- **Confidence:** high
- **Interpretation supported:** Performance is materially higher in symptomatic tissue-selected cohorts than in community or nonhemorrhagic samples
- **Not established:** Universal sensitivity or specificity across referral, memory, and screening settings

## Evidence relationships

### Supports: S001/R006

- **Population/model:** Symptomatic referral cohorts
- **Result:** Probable v2.0 achieved sensitivity 74.8-92.5% and specificity 81.5-95.0% across reported samples
- **Weight:** high
- **Limitation:** Tissue-selected symptomatic spectrum
- **Extraction:** full_text_extracted

### Supports: S012/R051

- **Population/model:** 134 ROSMAP MRI-autopsy participants
- **Result:** Sensitivity fell to 38.8% with specificity 83.5%
- **Weight:** high
- **Limitation:** Selected community autopsy subset
- **Extraction:** full_text_web_extracted

### Supports: S013/R052

- **Population/model:** 54 Mayo community/memory participants
- **Result:** Probable v2.0 sensitivity 28.6%, specificity 65.3%, AUC 0.47
- **Weight:** high
- **Limitation:** Small sample and wide uncertainty
- **Extraction:** full_text_web_extracted

## Synthesis judgment

- **Dimension scores:** directness 2/2; bias control 2/2; consistency 2/2; precision 2/2; transportability 2/2.
- **Key limitation:** Available community validations remain selected autopsy-MRI subsets
- **Most decisive next test:** Prospective multicentre validation across prespecified intended-use populations
