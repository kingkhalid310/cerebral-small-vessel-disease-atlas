# C001: Impaired perivascular clearance contributes to CAA

- **Domain:** mechanism
- **Confidence:** moderate
- **Interpretation supported:** Clearance failure is a biologically coherent contributor and may participate in feedback with vascular amyloid
- **Not established:** That impaired outward flow is the initiating cause in sporadic human CAA or that MRI-visible PVS directly measure clearance

## Evidence relationships

### Supports: S004/R012

- **Population/model:** 19 definite CAA and 5 control autopsies
- **Result:** EPVS burden tracked overlying cortical CAA and vessel smooth-muscle loss
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Cross-sectional and small; does not measure flow
