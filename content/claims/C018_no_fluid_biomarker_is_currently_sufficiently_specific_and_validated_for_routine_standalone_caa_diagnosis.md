# C018: No fluid biomarker is currently sufficiently specific and validated for routine standalone CAA diagnosis

- **Domain:** biomarker
- **Confidence:** moderate
- **Interpretation supported:** CSF and plasma measures remain research tools whose signals overlap with Alzheimer pathology
- **Not established:** That fluid measures have no future role in multimodal or treatment-risk models

## Evidence relationships

### Supports: R031

- **Population/model:** Symptomatic CAA versus controls/AD
- **Result:** CSF A-beta patterns differ on average but overlap limits individual diagnosis
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Small heterogeneous studies and clinical rather than pathology reference
- **Extraction:** citation_only

### Context: R032

- **Population/model:** Amyloid PET studies in sporadic CAA
- **Result:** PET can be sensitive to amyloid but parenchymal plaque signal limits specificity
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Tracer and comparator heterogeneity
- **Extraction:** citation_only

### Supports: R053

- **Population/model:** CAA fluid-biomarker literature through 2026
- **Result:** No fluid marker considered sufficiently specific and validated for routine standalone diagnosis
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Review conclusion awaits more pathology-anchored cohorts
- **Extraction:** abstract_extracted
