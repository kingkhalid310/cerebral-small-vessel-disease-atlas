# C016: Composite SVD scores summarize burden but do not identify etiology

- **Domain:** measurement
- **Confidence:** high
- **Interpretation supported:** Ordinal scores can stratify total visible burden and prognosis
- **Not established:** Continuous severity, lesion mechanism, equal biological weighting, or one underlying disease
