# C011: ARTS predicts pathology-linked arteriolosclerosis risk in older research cohorts

- **Domain:** biomarker
- **Confidence:** low
- **Interpretation supported:** ARTS shows approximately 0.8 discrimination under short MRI-death intervals in development/testing cohorts
- **Not established:** Clinical diagnostic accuracy in younger or representative populations, calibrated thresholds, or treatment responsiveness

## Evidence relationships

### Supports: S006/R017

- **Population/model:** 119 training and 79 pathology test participants

- **Limitation:** Small pathology sample and incomplete calibration
