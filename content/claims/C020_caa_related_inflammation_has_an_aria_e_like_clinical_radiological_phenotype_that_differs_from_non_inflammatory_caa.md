# C020: CAA-related inflammation has an ARIA-E-like clinical-radiological phenotype that differs from non-inflammatory CAA

- **Domain:** inflammatory CAA
- **Confidence:** moderate
- **Interpretation supported:** Asymmetric parenchymal/sulcal FLAIR abnormalities and gyral swelling strongly characterize referred CAA-ri cohorts
- **Not established:** That imaging alone always distinguishes CAA-ri from infection, neoplasm, PRES, vasculitis, or treatment-related ARIA

## Evidence relationships

### Supports: S019/R057

- **Population/model:** 378 reported CAA-ri cases
- **Result:** FLAIR abnormalities and lobar microbleeds were highly prevalent
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Heterogeneous retrospective evidence
- **Extraction:** abstract_extracted

### Supports: S021/R061

- **Population/model:** 37 CAA-ri versus 158 CAA
- **Result:** ARIA-E features were much more common in CAA-ri
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Referral cohort and incomplete pathology
- **Extraction:** full_text_web_extracted
