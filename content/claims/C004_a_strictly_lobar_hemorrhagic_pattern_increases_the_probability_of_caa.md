# C004: A strictly lobar hemorrhagic pattern increases the probability of CAA

- **Domain:** diagnosis
- **Confidence:** moderate
- **Interpretation supported:** Distribution is diagnostically informative in symptomatic populations when mimics and deep lesions are considered
- **Not established:** That every lobar CMB is caused by CAA or that performance transfers unchanged to screening populations

## Evidence relationships

### Supports: S001/R006

- **Population/model:** Symptomatic patients age >=50 with MRI and tissue
- **Result:** Boston v2.0 probable CAA showed strong specificity, especially in full-autopsy subset
- **Weight:** high
- **Limitation:** Tissue-selected referral spectrum
- **Extraction:** full_text_extracted

### Challenges: S003/R015

- **Population/model:** Three false-positive probable CAA cases
- **Result:** Seven confirmed lobar CMBs were linked to arteriolosclerosis without CAA
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Demonstrates possibility, not frequency
- **Extraction:** full_text_extracted

### Qualifies: S012/R051

- **Population/model:** 134 ROSMAP MRI-autopsy participants
- **Result:** Probable v2.0 sensitivity 38.8% and specificity 83.5%; cortical CMB count separated true from false positives
- **Weight:** high
- **Limitation:** Selected older MRI-autopsy subset
- **Extraction:** full_text_web_extracted

### Challenges: S013/R052

- **Population/model:** 54 community/memory MRI-autopsy participants
- **Result:** Probable v2.0 AUC 0.47 in nonhemorrhagic presentations
- **Weight:** high
- **Limitation:** Small and imprecise but directly tests transport
- **Extraction:** full_text_web_extracted

### Method: R046

- **Population/model:** Susceptibility MRI
- **Result:** CMB detection depends on sequence, mimics, size, and rating conventions
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Review rather than etiologic validation
- **Extraction:** citation_only

## Synthesis judgment

- **Dimension scores:** directness 2/2; bias control 1/2; consistency 1/2; precision 1/2; transportability 1/2.
- **Key limitation:** Arteriolosclerosis can produce lobar CMBs and accuracy is spectrum-dependent
- **Most decisive next test:** Large lesion-level MRI-pathology coregistration across symptomatic and community cohorts
