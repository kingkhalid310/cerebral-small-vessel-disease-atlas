# C022: Spontaneous CAA-related inflammation and treatment-related ARIA share vascular-amyloid, permeability, and inflammatory features but are not interchangeable entities

- **Domain:** mechanism and safety
- **Confidence:** moderate
- **Interpretation supported:** The overlap is a productive mechanistic analogy that informs detection and monitoring
- **Not established:** That the conditions have identical initiating events, natural history, risk factors, or optimal treatment

- **Result:** Workgroup links vascular amyloid, inflammation, permeability, ARIA-E and ARIA-H
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Mechanistic synthesis rather than direct comparison
- **Extraction:** full_text_web_extracted
