# C006: The multispot WMH pattern adds information for CAA classification

- **Domain:** diagnosis
- **Confidence:** low
- **Interpretation supported:** The pattern can contribute to Boston v2.0 classification
- **Not established:** That it is a strong standalone or pathology-specific CAA marker

## Evidence relationships

### Supports: S001/R006

- **Population/model:** Symptomatic tissue-referenced cohort
- **Result:** Multispot WMH was retained as one nonhemorrhagic v2.0 feature
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Feature is used only in combination
- **Extraction:** full_text_extracted

### Qualifies: S012/R051

- **Population/model:** 134 community MRI-autopsy participants
- **Result:** WMH multispot added less sensitivity than CSO-PVS
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Rating and cohort spectrum may affect performance
- **Extraction:** full_text_web_extracted

### Context: R042

- **Population/model:** 21 participants in exploratory PET study
- **Result:** Multispot lesions were associated with cerebrovascular amyloid burden
- **Weight:** low
- **Limitation:** Very small pilot; PET signal is not pathology-specific
- **Extraction:** citation_only
