# C005: Severe centrum-semiovale PVS add information for CAA classification

- **Domain:** diagnosis
- **Confidence:** moderate
- **Interpretation supported:** CSO-PVS can improve sensitivity when combined with hemorrhagic features in Boston v2.0
- **Not established:** That PVS are specific for CAA or directly quantify clearance

## Evidence relationships

### Supports: S001/R006

- **Population/model:** Symptomatic tissue-referenced cohort
- **Result:** Severe CSO-PVS contributed to the v2.0 rule combining hemorrhagic and white-matter features
- **Weight:** high
- **Limitation:** Incremental value is model- and population-dependent
- **Extraction:** full_text_extracted

### Supports: S004/R012

- **Population/model:** CAA autopsies and controls
- **Result:** MRI-visible PVS corresponded to histologic enlargement and overlying CAA
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Not a standalone diagnostic-accuracy study
- **Extraction:** full_text_extracted

- **Result:** CSO-PVS increased sensitivity more than the multispot WMH feature
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Overall sensitivity remained limited
