# C019: Cerebrovascular reactivity is a reproducible physiological correlate of SVD-related cognition and imaging progression, but is not etiologically specific

- **Domain:** physiological biomarker
- **Confidence:** moderate
- **Interpretation supported:** Multisite and longitudinal evidence supports CVR as a vascular-function marker related to cognition and later tissue injury
- **Not established:** That CVR identifies CAA or arteriolosclerosis, adds clinical utility beyond conventional MRI, or is treatment-responsive

## Evidence relationships

### Supports: S017/R054

- **Population/model:** 263 older adults across three MarkVCID sites
- **Result:** CVR-cognition associations reproduced across sites
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Concurrent association and no pathology reference
- **Extraction:** abstract_plus_web_extracted

### Supports: S018/R060

- **Population/model:** 163 minor-stroke participants with one-year MRI
- **Result:** Lower baseline CVR predicted WMH and PVS progression but not clinical outcomes
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Single region and SVD-enriched stroke population
- **Extraction:** full_text_web_extracted

## Synthesis judgment

- **Dimension scores:** directness 1/2; bias control 1/2; consistency 2/2; precision 1/2; transportability 1/2.
- **Key limitation:** No pathology-specific reference standard and nontrivial gas-challenge implementation
- **Most decisive next test:** Standardized multisite longitudinal CVR with pathology or validated etiologic reference, incremental-value analysis, and intervention responsiveness
