# C021: Regional amyloid PET patterns may add differential information for CAA versus AD, but current evidence does not support standalone pathology-specific diagnosis

- **Domain:** molecular imaging
- **Confidence:** low
- **Interpretation supported:** Group-level occipital-to-global and global retention differences are reproducible enough to justify prospective testing
- **Not established:** A validated patient-level threshold that separates vascular from parenchymal amyloid across tracers and disease stages

## Evidence relationships

### Supports: S020/R059

- **Population/model:** 16 imaging cohorts; 271 CAA participants
- **Result:** Regional and global PET group differences were detected
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Predominantly clinical CAA reference
- **Extraction:** full_text_web_extracted

### Qualifies: R032

- **Population/model:** Earlier amyloid PET literature
- **Result:** High amyloid positivity in older adults and AD limits specificity
- **Weight:** moderate
- **Limitation:** Older, smaller literature
- **Extraction:** citation_only
