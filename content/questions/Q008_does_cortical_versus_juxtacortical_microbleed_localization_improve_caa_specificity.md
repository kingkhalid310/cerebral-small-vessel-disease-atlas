# Q008: Does cortical versus juxtacortical microbleed localization improve CAA specificity?

- **Domain:** CAA diagnosis
- **Priority:** essential
- **Evidence status:** emerging
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Older adults with lobar microbleeds
- **Index/exposure:** Fine-grained lesion-to-cortex localization
- **Comparator:** Strictly lobar count alone
- **Reference standard:** Lesion-level histopathology
- **Outcome:** Incremental specificity and reproducibility

## Current synthesis

Histopathology suggests some false-positive lobar lesions arise from nonamyloid arteriolosclerosis.

## Missing piece

Automated reproducible localization and prospective pathology validation.

## Decisive design

Lesion-matched MRI-histology study with competing arteriolosclerosis
