# Q030: How do vascular amyloid and AD neuropathologic change interact to produce cognition?

- **Domain:** mixed pathology
- **Priority:** high
- **Evidence status:** emerging
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Older adults with autopsy and longitudinal cognition
- **Index/exposure:** CAA and ADNC jointly
- **Comparator:** Each pathology alone
- **Reference standard:** Standardized pathology and cognitive trajectories
- **Outcome:** Additive versus interactive effects

## Current synthesis

The pathologies often coexist; shared amyloid biology does not imply identical compartmental effects.

## Missing piece

Large multiregion pathology cohort with preregistered interaction and mediation models.

## Decisive design

Longitudinal autopsy cohort with independent replication
