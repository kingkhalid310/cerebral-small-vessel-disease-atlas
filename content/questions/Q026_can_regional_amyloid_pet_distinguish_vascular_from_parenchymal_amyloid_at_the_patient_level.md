# Q026: Can regional amyloid PET distinguish vascular from parenchymal amyloid at the patient level?

- **Domain:** molecular imaging
- **Priority:** essential
- **Evidence status:** emerging
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Suspected CAA, AD, and mixed disease
- **Index/exposure:** Regional tracer ratios and spatial models
- **Comparator:** Global amyloid positivity
- **Reference standard:** Autopsy compartment-specific amyloid
- **Outcome:** Threshold accuracy and transportability

## Current synthesis

Group-level regional differences exist, but pathology-specific patient thresholds are not validated.

## Missing piece

Prospective multi-tracer PET-to-autopsy validation.

## Decisive design

Prespecified multicentre diagnostic study
