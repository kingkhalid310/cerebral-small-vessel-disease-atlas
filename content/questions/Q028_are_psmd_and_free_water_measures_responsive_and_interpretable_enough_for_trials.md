# Q028: Are PSMD and free-water measures responsive and interpretable enough for trials?

- **Domain:** quantitative MRI
- **Priority:** high
- **Evidence status:** emerging
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** VCID and cSVD intervention cohorts
- **Index/exposure:** PSMD and white-matter free water
- **Comparator:** Conventional MRI and clinical scales
- **Reference standard:** Change reliability and meaningful outcome
- **Outcome:** Treatment sensitivity and minimal important change

## Current synthesis

Technical and biological validation is advancing, but treatment responsiveness is not established.

## Missing piece

Prospective trial-embedded repeatability and responsiveness study.

## Decisive design

Multisite intervention study with prespecified change thresholds
