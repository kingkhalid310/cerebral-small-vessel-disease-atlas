# Q013: What ante-mortem MRI phenotype best predicts pathology-confirmed brain arteriolosclerosis?

- **Domain:** arteriolosclerosis diagnosis
- **Priority:** essential
- **Evidence status:** critical_gap
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Older adults with MRI before death
- **Index/exposure:** Deep microbleeds, lacunes, WMH topography, diffusion, physiology, and demographics
- **Comparator:** Clinical vascular risks and conventional MRI burden
- **Reference standard:** Standardized multiregion pathology
- **Outcome:** External discrimination, calibration, and net reclassification

## Current synthesis

No accepted MRI diagnostic criteria exist; current features are candidate phenotypes.

## Missing piece

Large representative MRI-autopsy derivation and locked external validation.

## Decisive design

Two-cohort prospective or nested autopsy prediction study
