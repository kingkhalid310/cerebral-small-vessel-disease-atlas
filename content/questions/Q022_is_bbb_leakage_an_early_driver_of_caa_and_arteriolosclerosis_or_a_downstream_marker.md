# Q022: Is BBB leakage an early driver of CAA and arteriolosclerosis or a downstream marker?

- **Domain:** mechanism
- **Priority:** high
- **Evidence status:** emerging
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Human tissue and in-vivo SVD stages
- **Index/exposure:** DCE-MRI and molecular leakage markers
- **Comparator:** Vessel pathology grade and inflammation
- **Reference standard:** Spatial pathology and longitudinal change
- **Outcome:** Timing and mediation

## Current synthesis

Leakage co-localizes with vessel injury and inflammation, but temporal direction in humans is uncertain.

## Missing piece

Prospective serial permeability imaging anchored to pathology or strong etiologic models.

## Decisive design

Spatial longitudinal imaging-pathology study
