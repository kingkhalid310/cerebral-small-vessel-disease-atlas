# Q020: Is impaired perivascular clearance an initiating cause, amplifier, or consequence of CAA?

- **Domain:** mechanism
- **Priority:** high
- **Evidence status:** emerging
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Preclinical, hereditary, and sporadic CAA
- **Index/exposure:** Direct or proxy clearance measures
- **Comparator:** Amyloid burden and vascular function
- **Reference standard:** Longitudinal compartment-specific transport
- **Outcome:** Temporal and causal ordering

## Current synthesis

Clearance impairment is coherent and spatially associated, but human direction and flux remain uncertain.

## Missing piece

Direct human transport measurement before and during amyloid accumulation.

## Decisive design

Longitudinal hereditary/sporadic multimodal transport study
