# Q003: What minimum MRI protocol is required to phenotype hemorrhagic and nonhemorrhagic cSVD reproducibly?

- **Domain:** measurement
- **Priority:** high
- **Evidence status:** bounded
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Adults undergoing cSVD research MRI
- **Index/exposure:** T1, T2, FLAIR, DWI, susceptibility, and optional quantitative sequences
- **Comparator:** Reduced and heterogeneous protocols
- **Reference standard:** STRIVE-aligned acquisition and expert image review
- **Outcome:** Feature detectability, reliability, and missingness

## Current synthesis

Core structural and susceptibility-sensitive sequences are necessary, but protocol effects remain substantial.

## Missing piece

Prospective sequence-ablation evidence across scanners.

## Decisive design

Multivendor scan-rescan study with blinded sequence-ablation reading
