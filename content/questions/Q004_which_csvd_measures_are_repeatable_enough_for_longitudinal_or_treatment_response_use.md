# Q004: Which cSVD measures are repeatable enough for longitudinal or treatment-response use?

- **Domain:** measurement
- **Priority:** high
- **Evidence status:** emerging
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Stable and progressing cSVD cohorts
- **Index/exposure:** Visual ratings and quantitative biomarkers
- **Comparator:** Scan-rescan and cross-scanner conditions
- **Reference standard:** Prespecified repeatability and smallest-detectable-change standards
- **Outcome:** Reliability and sensitivity to change

## Current synthesis

Some tools are repeatable, but repeatability does not imply biological or clinical validity.

## Missing piece

Common change thresholds and error decomposition.

## Decisive design

Multisite scan-rescan plus longitudinal progression study
