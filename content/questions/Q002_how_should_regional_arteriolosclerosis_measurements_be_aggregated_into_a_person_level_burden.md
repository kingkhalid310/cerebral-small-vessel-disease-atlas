# Q002: How should regional arteriolosclerosis measurements be aggregated into a person-level burden?

- **Domain:** pathology
- **Priority:** essential
- **Evidence status:** critical_gap
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Autopsy cohorts with multiregion sampling
- **Index/exposure:** Regional vessel pathology
- **Comparator:** Maximum, mean, weighted, latent, and spatial-pattern scores
- **Reference standard:** Whole-brain sampling model
- **Outcome:** Person-level validity and prediction

## Current synthesis

No universally accepted aggregation rule exists.

## Missing piece

Sampling theory for spatially heterogeneous disease.

## Decisive design

Dense multiregion sampling with bootstrap stability and external outcome validation
