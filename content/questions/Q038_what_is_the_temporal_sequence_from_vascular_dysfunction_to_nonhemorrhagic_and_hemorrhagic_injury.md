# Q038: What is the temporal sequence from vascular dysfunction to nonhemorrhagic and hemorrhagic injury?

- **Domain:** progression
- **Priority:** essential
- **Evidence status:** critical_gap
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Preclinical through symptomatic CAA and arteriolosclerosis
- **Index/exposure:** Repeated physiology, barrier, diffusion, lesion, and amyloid measures
- **Comparator:** Cross-sectional stage models
- **Reference standard:** Longitudinal transitions and pathology
- **Outcome:** Ordering and transition rates

## Current synthesis

A staged framework is plausible, but individual-level transition evidence remains incomplete.

## Missing piece

Dense longitudinal multimodal sampling beginning before symptoms.

## Decisive design

Hereditary-plus-sporadic natural-history platform
