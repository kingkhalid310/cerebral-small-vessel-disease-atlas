# Q015: Which lacune locations and morphologies are most informative for arteriolosclerosis?

- **Domain:** arteriolosclerosis diagnosis
- **Priority:** high

## Review frame

- **Population:** Adults with lacunes
- **Index/exposure:** Location, shape, cavity characteristics, and surrounding tissue
- **Comparator:** Lacune count alone
- **Reference standard:** Regional arteriolar pathology and infarct mechanism adjudication
- **Outcome:** Specificity and reliability

## Current synthesis

Lacunes are heterogeneous downstream injuries and current descriptors are not pathology-specific.

## Missing piece
