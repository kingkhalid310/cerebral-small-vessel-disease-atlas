# Q032: What links arteriolosclerosis to hippocampal sclerosis and LATE-related pathology?

- **Domain:** mixed pathology
- **Priority:** high
- **Evidence status:** emerging
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Oldest-old autopsy cohorts
- **Index/exposure:** Regional arteriolosclerosis burden
- **Comparator:** Other vascular and neurodegenerative pathologies
- **Reference standard:** Hippocampal sclerosis and TDP-43 staging
- **Outcome:** Independent association and mechanism

## Current synthesis

Associations exist, but shared age, vascular injury, and selective vulnerability remain competing explanations.

## Missing piece

Longitudinal and spatial mediation with replication.

## Decisive design

Independent multiregion autopsy cohorts
