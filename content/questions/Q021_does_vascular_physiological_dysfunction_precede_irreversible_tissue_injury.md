# Q021: Does vascular physiological dysfunction precede irreversible tissue injury?

- **Domain:** mechanism
- **Priority:** high
- **Evidence status:** emerging
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Individuals across preclinical and clinical SVD stages
- **Index/exposure:** CVR, pulsatility, perfusion, and barrier measures
- **Comparator:** Structural MRI markers
- **Reference standard:** Longitudinal lesion progression
- **Outcome:** Temporal precedence and mediation

## Current synthesis

CVR can precede WMH/PVS progression in SVD, but etiologic and causal specificity remain unresolved.

## Missing piece

Repeated physiology and imaging with causal mediation and intervention.

## Decisive design

Multisite longitudinal cohort with repeated measures
