# Q035: How does APOE modify CAA burden, hemorrhage, inflammation, and ARIA risk?

- **Domain:** genetics
- **Priority:** high
- **Evidence status:** bounded
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Sporadic CAA and anti-amyloid-treated populations
- **Index/exposure:** APOE genotype
- **Comparator:** Clinical/imaging risk factors without genotype
- **Reference standard:** Pathology and adjudicated outcomes
- **Outcome:** Incremental and interaction effects

## Current synthesis

APOE contributes meaningful risk information in some contexts but is neither necessary nor sufficient.

## Missing piece

Ancestry-diverse calibrated models with treatment interactions.

## Decisive design

Large prospective real-world registry linked to standardized MRI
