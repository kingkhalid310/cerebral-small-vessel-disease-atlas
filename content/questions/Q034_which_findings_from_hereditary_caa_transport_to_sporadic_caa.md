# Q034: Which findings from hereditary CAA transport to sporadic CAA?

- **Domain:** genetics
- **Priority:** high
- **Evidence status:** emerging
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Hereditary and sporadic CAA cohorts
- **Index/exposure:** Shared imaging, fluid, physiology, and pathology measures
- **Comparator:** Within-form trajectories
- **Reference standard:** Harmonized longitudinal phenotypes
- **Outcome:** Transportability and temporal sequence

## Current synthesis

Hereditary CAA is mechanistically informative but differs in age, mutation, penetrance, and co-pathology.

## Missing piece

## Decisive design
