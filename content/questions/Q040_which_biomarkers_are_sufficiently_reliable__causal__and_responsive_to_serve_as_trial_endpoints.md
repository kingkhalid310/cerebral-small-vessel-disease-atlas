# Q040: Which biomarkers are sufficiently reliable, causal, and responsive to serve as trial endpoints?

- **Domain:** trials
- **Priority:** essential
- **Evidence status:** critical_gap
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Intervention-ready CAA and arteriolosclerosis cohorts
- **Index/exposure:** Imaging, physiological, fluid, and digital-pathology biomarkers
- **Comparator:** Clinical outcomes and conventional MRI
- **Reference standard:** Treatment-induced change linked to patient benefit
- **Outcome:** Responsiveness and surrogacy

## Current synthesis

No arteriolosclerosis biomarker and few CAA biomarkers meet the full chain from repeatability to clinical surrogacy.

## Missing piece

Trial-embedded qualification with prespecified causal and clinical validation.

## Decisive design

Multistage biomarker-qualification program
