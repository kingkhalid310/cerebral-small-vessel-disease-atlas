# Q018: Does ARTS add clinically meaningful information beyond conventional measures?

- **Domain:** arteriolosclerosis biomarker
- **Priority:** essential
- **Evidence status:** critical_gap
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Older adults with MRI
- **Index/exposure:** ARTS added to baseline model
- **Comparator:** Baseline model alone
- **Reference standard:** Pathology, cognition, vascular events
- **Outcome:** Incremental value and net benefit

## Current synthesis

Associations and technical repeatability do not yet establish incremental clinical utility.

## Missing piece

Head-to-head incremental-value and decision-curve analysis.

## Decisive design

External cohort with locked model and prespecified utility thresholds
