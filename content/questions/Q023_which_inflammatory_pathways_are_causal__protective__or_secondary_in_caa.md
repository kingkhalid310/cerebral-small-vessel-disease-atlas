# Q023: Which inflammatory pathways are causal, protective, or secondary in CAA?

- **Domain:** mechanism
- **Priority:** high
- **Evidence status:** emerging
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Sporadic CAA, CAA-ri, ARIA, and models
- **Index/exposure:** Immune cells, complement, cytokines, antibodies
- **Comparator:** Amyloid burden and barrier injury
- **Reference standard:** Intervention-sensitive mechanistic endpoints
- **Outcome:** Causal contribution and heterogeneity

## Current synthesis

Inflammation participates in CAA-ri and ARIA; its role in non-inflammatory CAA is less defined.

## Missing piece

Mechanistically targeted longitudinal human studies with treatment perturbation.

## Decisive design

Parallel CAA/CAA-ri/ARIA immune-phenotyping study
