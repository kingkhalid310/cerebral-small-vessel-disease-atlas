# Q014: How specific are deep microbleeds for nonamyloid arteriolosclerosis?

- **Domain:** arteriolosclerosis diagnosis
- **Priority:** essential
- **Evidence status:** critical_gap
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Older adults with deep or mixed microbleeds
- **Index/exposure:** Deep lesion burden and microanatomic localization
- **Comparator:** Lobar lesions and vascular risks
- **Reference standard:** Lesion- and person-level pathology
- **Outcome:** Etiologic likelihood ratios

## Current synthesis

Deep distribution is compatible with perforator disease but is not a direct pathology diagnosis.

## Missing piece

Lesion-matched pathology with CAA and mixed-disease controls.

## Decisive design

Multiregion lesion-to-vessel histology study
