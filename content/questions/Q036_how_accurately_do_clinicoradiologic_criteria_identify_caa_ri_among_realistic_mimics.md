# Q036: How accurately do clinicoradiologic criteria identify CAA-ri among realistic mimics?

- **Domain:** inflammatory CAA
- **Priority:** essential
- **Evidence status:** bounded
- **Last searched:** 2026-08-18

## Review frame

- **Population:** Adults with subacute encephalopathy, seizures, or asymmetric FLAIR lesions
- **Index/exposure:** CAA-ri criteria
- **Comparator:** Expert impression and alternative diagnoses
- **Reference standard:** Biopsy or adjudicated longitudinal diagnosis
- **Outcome:** Accuracy and biopsy avoidance

## Current synthesis

Criteria perform well in selected cohorts, but mimic-rich external accuracy is incompletely defined.

## Missing piece

Prospective blinded study with infection, neoplasm, PRES, and vasculitis controls.

## Decisive design

Multicentre diagnostic cohort
