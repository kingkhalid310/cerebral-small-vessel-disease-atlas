# Research and medical disclaimer

This repository is an educational and research resource. It is not medical advice, a clinical guideline, a regulated medical device, or a substitute for qualified clinical judgment.

The records summarize and critically organize published evidence. They may contain errors, omissions, provisional interpretations, and judgments made by a single curator. Diagnostic criteria and biomarkers must be interpreted within their intended populations, acquisition requirements, reference standards, and validation settings.

Do not use this repository to diagnose, exclude, monitor, or treat an individual patient. Urgent or patient-specific questions should be directed to an appropriately qualified clinician.

The repository contains no patient-level data and accepts no protected health information. Do not submit clinical records, identifiers, images, or other sensitive information through issues or pull requests.
