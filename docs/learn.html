Learning pathways

From first principles to research-grade questions.

You do not need to begin with criteria or memorized imaging signs. Begin with vessel compartments, mechanisms of injury, and inference—then build toward diagnosis and study design.

Pathway 03 · criteria and biomarkers

Ask what each tool has actually proved

A tool can be technically repeatable and biologically interesting while still lacking diagnostic specificity, transportability, or decision utility.

STRIVE-2

Terminology and reporting standards for neuroimaging features—not an etiologic diagnostic rule.

Boston criteria v2.0

Accuracy and transportability across symptomatic, cognitive, and community contexts.

ARTS

A promising pathology-linked arteriolosclerosis score with an incomplete external-validation chain.

ARIA framework

Monitoring and risk reasoning in the setting of anti-amyloid treatment.

Pathway 04 · become a research designer

Turn uncertainty into a testable program

01

Choose a critical gap

Start with the current answer, missing piece, and decisive design rather than an unbounded topic.

 02

Try to falsify the idea

Inspect strongest support, strongest challenge, alternatives, falsifiers, and the proposed experiment.

 03

Audit independence

Check recruitment, reused cohorts, reference standards, and bias before counting publications as replication.
