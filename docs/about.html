About the guide

A field map for people who want to think, not merely memorize.

The guide was created to help a new researcher develop deep, evidence-calibrated knowledge while preserving enough structure to identify missing pieces and design better studies.

Who it is for

The primary audience is a researcher entering cerebral small vessel disease who wants to progress from foundational concepts to hypothesis-generating work. Secondary audiences include trainees, neurologists, neuropathologists, neuroimaging scientists, methodologists, data scientists, and collaborators who need shared definitions and evidence boundaries.

Scope

The current emphasis is cerebral amyloid angiopathy, brain arteriolosclerosis, mixed cerebral small vessel disease, diagnostic criteria, neuroimaging, neuropathology, physiology, biomarkers, cognition, and research design. The scope can expand without collapsing different etiologies into one undifferentiated “vascular” category.

What makes it different

  • Research questions carry an explicit current answer, missing piece, and decisive design.
  • Claims are connected to evidence through typed relationships.
  • Cohort lineage is tracked to reduce false impressions of independent replication.
  • Diagnostic tools are graded across the entire validation chain.
  • Hypotheses include strongest challenges, alternatives, and falsifiers.
  • Public presentation is generated from maintainable structured data.

Citation

Cite the repository release when using its organization or synthesis, and cite the original scholarly sources for scientific claims. Machine-readable citation metadata are available in CITATION.cff.

Reuse

No public reuse license has yet been selected. The repository owner should choose separate content and code licenses before public launch. Referenced publications and third-party tools retain their own rights.

Corrections and contributions

Corrections should identify the affected record, the problem, and supporting evidence. Never submit protected health information, clinical records, or copyrighted full text.

Medical disclaimer
