Methodology

Transparent about both evidence and absence of evidence.

The guide is structured to resist the most common errors in this field: overinterpreting associations, confusing repeatability with validity, ignoring mixed disease, and counting reused cohorts as independent replication.

What this is

A curated scoping evidence system organized around sources, studies, evidence edges, claims, research questions, cohorts, tools, diagnostic profiles, hypotheses, and educational cases.

What this is not

  • Not a completed systematic review or meta-analysis.
  • Not a consensus guideline or formal diagnostic-criteria development process.
  • Not patient-level decision support.
  • Not proof that absence from the guide means absence from the literature.

The evidence chain

Each major claim is separated from the studies used to support or challenge it. An evidence edge records the relationship—support, qualification, challenge, context, or method—rather than assuming every citation provides equivalent confirmation.

The validation chain

  1. Technical validity: can the measure be acquired and reproduced?
  2. Biological validity: does it reflect the proposed tissue or physiological construct?
  3. Diagnostic accuracy: does it distinguish the target disease against an appropriate reference standard?
  4. Prognostic validity: does it predict meaningful outcomes?
  5. Incremental value: does it add beyond available information?
  6. Transportability: does performance survive new scanners, settings, ancestries, and disease mixtures?
  7. Clinical utility: does using it improve decisions or outcomes?

Question status

StatusMeaning
BoundedA useful answer exists within a defined population or context, although limitations remain.
EmergingEvidence is coherent enough to support a provisional synthesis, but validation or causal ordering remains incomplete.
Critical gapA decision-relevant element—often reference-standard accuracy, external validation, temporal evidence, or utility—is missing.

Editorial updating

Literature updating is intentionally manual. A curator discovers and screens a source, records what level of text was assessed, extracts the study, connects it to claims and questions, updates conclusions only when warranted, rebuilds the site catalog, and runs validation.

Interpretation rule

A radiology-report term, imaging distribution, composite score, or biological association may be useful for candidate discovery without being sufficiently specific to diagnose pathology.

Known limitations

The current appraisal is largely single-reviewer, coverage is intentionally selective, some records rely on abstracts rather than full text, and no formal certainty-of-evidence process has been completed. These limitations are data fields, not footnotes.
